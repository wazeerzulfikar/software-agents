Matchmaking: Yenta

Leonard Foner, 1995–1999  /  Yenta 0.7.0

Yenta reads your mail and files to work out what you are interested in. Then, without any central server, it finds other people’s Yentas that share those interests, especially the rare ones, and introduces you.

Use itTick Start finding interests, press Make changes and watch the doors/read your messages and reply
