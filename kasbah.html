Transactions: Kasbah

Anthony Chavez & Pattie Maes, 1996

Kasbah is a marketplace where you do not haggle yourself: you tell an agent what to sell, what to ask and how low it may go, and it bargains with other people’s buying agents for you.

Use itPress Create Agent at the foot of the form. Left blank, it sells The Joshua Tree by U2 for $12.
