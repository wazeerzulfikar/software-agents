HOMR
Upendra Shardanand, Max Metral & Pattie Maes, 1994–1996

HOMR is a personal critic for music. It compares your ratings with everyone else’s, finds the people whose taste is most like yours, and tells you what they love that you have not heard yet. The screen is from its later life as Firefly.

Use itRate a few of these artists from the menus, press Submit and Send More, and see how the next five change. Click an artist, or people, to see why.
